Helloooooooooo I would like to start a discussion about Meteor’s current release cadence, especially regarding release candidates and small patches. My feeling is that we sometimes wait too long before making relatively small improvements available to users. A minor fix or quick win may be merged, but then remain bundled into a much larger release candidate, or even the next minor release, and effectively stay unavailable for several weeks or months.
