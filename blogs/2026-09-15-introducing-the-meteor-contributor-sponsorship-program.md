Meteor is starting a sponsorship program for the people who keep this project moving. Each quarter, we sponsor one trusted community contributor, as a way of recognizing work that has been happening for free for a long time. Where the money comes from Earlier this year we added a Sponsors section to the Meteor repository, and companies started backing the project: Galaxy Cloud , CodeRabbit and Input Logic were the first.
