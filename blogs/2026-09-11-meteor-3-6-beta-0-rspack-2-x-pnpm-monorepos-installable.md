Meteor 3.5 brought MongoDB Change Streams, DDP session resumption, pluggable transports, and Node.js 24. The 3.5.x patches then hardened the runtime, Rspack builds, and reconnection behavior. Meteor 3.6-beta.0 is the first beta of the next minor release, and it is a big one for the modern build stack: Rspack 2.x with automatic migration, a pnpm monorepo skeleton with workspace-aware dependency installs, an installable Blaze PWA skeleton, an interactive meteor add , and packages and projects straight from Git.
